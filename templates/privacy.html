How we protect it

We use reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect your data. Card details stay with Stripe and never reach our servers, our admin areas are password protected, and we limit who can access the data. No system is completely secure.

If something goes wrong with your data

We keep a record of every security incident. If an incident is likely to put people's rights and freedoms at risk, we report it to the Information Commissioner's Office within 72 hours of becoming aware of it, where we can. If it is likely to cause a high risk to you, we will also tell you without undue delay.

Your rights

To use any of these rights, email us{% if site.contact %} at {{ site.contact }}{% endif %}. We will reply in line with data protection law, normally within one month.
