Reviews policy

Last updated {{ site.updated }}.

  • Who can review. Only our customers. Each customer gets a private, one-time link. Reviews cannot be posted any other way.
  • We publish every genuine review. Positive or negative, we publish it. We never edit the wording of a review.
  • What we remove. We only remove reviews that are spam, abusive, or not based on real use of {{ site.name }}. We also remove a reviewer's name, or the review, if they withdraw their agreement to publication. We write down the reason each time. We never remove a review because it is critical.
  • No rewards. We do not pay or reward anyone for a review. If that ever changes, any reward will be stated next to the review.
  • Ratings. The star rating shown is the plain average of every published review. We do not adjust it.
  • Invitations. We invite all customers to review, not a chosen few.

If you think a review on this site is fake or misleading, tell us{% if site.contact %} at {{ site.contact }}{% endif %} and we will look into it.
