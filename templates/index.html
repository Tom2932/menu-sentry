Questions

Which menus can you read?

Public web pages and PDF menus where the prices are written as text. We do not cover delivery apps. We only read what a competitor publishes on their own website, and we respect website access instructions such as robots.txt.

How do I choose my competitors?

After you sign up, email us the names of the restaurants you want to follow and a link to each one's menu.

What if a competitor's menu cannot be read?

Most online menus work. Some sites show their menu as a photo or in a way a program cannot read. We check every competitor when we set you up and tell you if one cannot be used.

How often is the report sent?

We aim to send one report a week, by email. If we send you no report for a whole month for reasons within our control, we refund that month. See the terms.

Is {{ site.name }} for businesses only?

Yes. It is for restaurants, cafes and other businesses. By signing up you confirm you are buying for the purposes of your business.

You keep access until the end of your current paid billing period, and cancelling stops the next renewal. Payments already made for the current period are not refundable unless the terms or the law say otherwise. See the terms.

What if a payment fails?

We may pause your reports until the payment is made. If we fail to send your reports for a month for reasons within our control, we refund that month's payment.

How is payment handled?

By Stripe, a payment company used by millions of businesses. We never see or store your card number.
